Information we collect

We collect contact information that parents and guardians provide at registration or at the front desk, such as name and mobile phone number, to communicate about your child's care. Protected health information is handled under our HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices.

Jackson Heights Pediatrics SMS Program

Text messaging is optional. You do not have to agree to receive texts to receive care, register as a patient, or book appointments. Only if you choose to opt in (by checking an optional, unchecked box on our intake form, or by saying yes to staff after hearing the disclosure), we may send text messages to your mobile number about the Jackson Heights Pediatrics SMS Program, such as post-visit thank-you messages with a link to share feedback.

  • Message frequency varies; you may receive up to 4 messages per month.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for help, or call (917) 832-6562 or email jacksonheightspediatrics@outlook.com.
  • Consent to receive texts is not a condition of receiving care.

Mobile information and messaging consent — no third-party sharing

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except with service providers (such as our text messaging platform) that help us deliver messages on our behalf and that may not use that data for any other purpose.

How we use this information

We use your mobile number only to send the SMS Program messages you opted into, to honor STOP/HELP requests, and to operate and improve the program. We do not use mobile opt-in data for unrelated marketing by third parties.

SMS Program terms

Full program terms, including opt-out, help, frequency, rates, and carrier liability, are in our SMS Terms & Conditions. How to opt in (optional intake-form box and verbal script): SMS Opt-In.
