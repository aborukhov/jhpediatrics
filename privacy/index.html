Information we collect

We collect contact information that parents and guardians provide at registration or at the front desk, such as name and mobile phone number, to communicate about your child's care. Protected health information is handled under our HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices.

Text messaging (SMS)

Text messaging is optional. You do not have to agree to receive texts to receive care, register as a patient, or book appointments. Only if you choose to opt in (by checking an optional, unchecked box on our form or by saying yes to staff after hearing the disclosure), we may send text messages to your mobile number, such as post-visit thank-you messages with a link to share feedback. Message frequency varies, up to 4 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time or HELP for help, or call (917) 832-6562. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of receiving care.

We do not share, sell, rent, or provide your mobile phone number, SMS opt-in data, or messaging consent to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except service providers (such as our text messaging platform) that help us deliver messages and may not use the data for any other purpose.
