Texting is completely optional. Consent to receive text messages is NOT required to receive care, register as a patient, or book appointments. Declining changes nothing: families who do not opt in receive exactly the same care and service.

1. Patient intake form – optional SMS consent section (sample)

This is how the optional SMS consent section appears on our patient intake form. The box is never pre-checked, and the form can be completed and submitted without checking it.

Text Message Consent (Optional) — Jackson Heights Pediatrics SMS Program

Mobile phone number:

See our Privacy Policy (states we do not share mobile numbers or messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes) and SMS Terms.

2. Front-desk verbal opt-in script (verbatim)

“Would you like to receive optional text messages from Jackson Heights Pediatrics, such as a thank-you and feedback request after your child's visit? Up to 4 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Saying yes is not required to receive care or book appointments.”

A parent/guardian is opted in only if they say yes after hearing this disclosure or check the optional box above. Staff then turn on text messaging in the patient's chart. If they say no or do not check the box, nothing else changes.

3. After opting in

  • Program: Jackson Heights Pediatrics SMS Program (post-visit thank-you and feedback requests).
  • Frequency: up to 4 messages per month.
  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out; reply HELP for help or call (917) 832-6562 / email jacksonheightspediatrics@outlook.com.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
