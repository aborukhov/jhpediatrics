Jackson Heights Pediatrics SMS Program Terms

This SMS program is optional. Consent to receive texts is not required to receive care, register as a patient, or book appointments. Parents/guardians who choose to opt in (an optional, unchecked box on our form, or a verbal yes after a disclosure from staff) receive post-visit thank-you and feedback-request text messages from Jackson Heights Pediatrics. Message frequency varies, up to 4 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help, or call (917) 832-6562. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. See our Privacy Policy.
