This SMS program is optional. Consent to receive text messages is not required to receive care, register as a patient, create an account, or book appointments. Declining does not change the care you receive.

Program name and description

Program name: Jackson Heights Pediatrics SMS Program

Description: Parents/guardians who choose to opt in receive post-visit thank-you and feedback-request text messages from Jackson Heights Pediatrics (Ilyayeva Irina Pediatrics PC).

How to opt in

Consent is collected only through a voluntary, program-specific action:

  1. Intake form: an optional, never-pre-checked SMS consent checkbox on the patient intake form; or
  2. Verbal: saying yes after staff read the disclosure script.

See the public sample form and verbatim script: https://aborukhov.github.io/jhpediatrics/opt-in/

Message frequency

Message frequency varies. You may receive up to 4 messages per month.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out (STOP) and help (HELP)

Reply STOP to cancel and stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help. You may also call (917) 832-6562 or email jacksonheightspediatrics@outlook.com.

Mobile information — no third-party sharing

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Opt-in data is used only to deliver this program's messages and to honor opt-out and help requests. See our Privacy Policy.

Carrier liability

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
