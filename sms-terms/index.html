Jackson Heights Pediatrics SMS Program Terms

Parents/guardians who opt in at registration or at the front desk receive post-visit thank-you and feedback-request text messages from Jackson Heights Pediatrics. Message frequency varies, up to 4 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help, or call (917) 832-6562. Consent is not a condition of care. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. See our Privacy Policy.
